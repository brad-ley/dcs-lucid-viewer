Welcome!¶

ATX245S camera equipped with the IMX530 sensor is a GigE Vision and GenICam compliant camera capable of over 1.2 GB/s data transfer rates (10 Gbps in Short Reach Mode), allowing for high resolutions and frame rates over standard copper Ethernet cables up to 25 meters. It features robust M8 IO connector, M12 interface connector with PoE and and Active Sensor Alignment for superior optical performance.

The ATX245S camera is designed for industrial applications such as factory automation and inspection, autonomous robotics, logistics, food and beverage, medical, biometrics and more.
